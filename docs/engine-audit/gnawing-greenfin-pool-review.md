# Gnawing Greenfin: complete frozen random-Murloc pool

`EDR_999` gets a random Murloc. Random generation excludes the creator and its canonical aliases, as required by Blizzard's [18.0.1 hotfix](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/hearthstone/t/1801-server-hotfix/39314). Discovering an existing card from a physical deck is a separate exception. Canonical alias exclusion is also supported by the [28.4 correction for alternate versions](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24046221). The production contract is restricted to the exact all-class, nonhistorical minion/Murloc request. It does not approve other tribe filters, cost ranges, historical pools or general minion pools.

The literal membership is reviewed against every collectible record in the frozen Standard catalog, patch 36.6.0.251952. All 22 pool identities have ordinary registered bodies once Greenfin is admitted; Greenfin has 21 eligible outcomes after creator exclusion. The membership includes the All-type Crater Experiment, dual-type minions, Inspector Murloc Holmes and Greenfin itself. Noncollectible tokens and historical cards do not belong to this Standard contract. No outcome is removed because of its implementation status.

Paid generation checks select each eligible outcome to verify its physical hand insertion. Every outcome also has a paid-play check through the ordinary registry. Further checks cover identical membership across all eleven hero classes, exact-request boundaries, creator exclusion before RNG, explicit creator provenance, full-hand behavior, and rejection before mutation if a reviewed member is unavailable. These checks establish local runtime paths, not independent client certification of every outcome's shared interactions.

Generation planning now distinguishes live recipes from remaining staged recipes and records reviewed membership only when the explicit contract exactly equals the full frozen candidate set. Broad pools remain unreviewed and fail closed. Delayed attached effects must supply reviewed creator provenance before admission; their host entity is not used as an inferred creator. Existing staged Follow and attached-Deathrattle fixtures remain controlled scenarios, not admission evidence.
