# Petal Peddler

EDR_889 uses the existing end-of-owner-turn pipeline and `buff_random_other` operation, extended with an optional tribe selector. Its own entity is excluded even though Petal Peddler is a Dragon. Other friendly Dragons, dual-tribe Dragons and ALL-type minions are eligible through the common tribe matcher. Opponents and untyped minions are excluded. The original unfiltered operation remains available to its existing cards.

Six fixtures cover ownership and tribe filtering, self exclusion, Silence and opponent turns, ALL-type eligibility, two Peddlers buffing each other, and exactly one selection among multiple eligible targets. The pinned card record supplies the rule text. General end-trigger ordering still requires independent client conformance evidence.
