# Consumption

CORE_CATA_007 uses the pinned two-Blood-rune card record. Its shared operation samples up to two distinct enemy minions, applies spell-scaled damage in a damage batch, records lethal victims, then uses the existing settlement boundary before drawing once per lethal victim. With zero targets it is a legal no-op; a lone minion is hit once. Draws stop if the hero dies.

Eight fixtures cover two kills, one/zero targets, Divine Shield, Spell Damage, distinct selection, friendly/hero exclusion, Reborn and lethal fatigue. Reborn creates a different entity and does not erase the original victim's death. General damage/death-trigger ordering and effects that rescue or replace a lethally damaged minion remain subject to independent client conformance work.
