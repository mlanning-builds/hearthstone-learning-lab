# Genn fixed-family review

Scope: frozen patch 36.6.0.251952. `CATA_615` is provisionally connected to the ordinary candidate registry, with its exact Worgen token, eleven literal upgraded power records, `AT_132_ROGUEt` and the distinct `HERO_11bp2t` Frenzied Ghoul. It does not require a random collectible pool. The upgraded Death Knight power summons the printed 2/1 Frenzied Ghoul, not a buffed 1/1 Frail Ghoul. This distinction preserves its identity and 2/1 printed stats through Silence and returning to hand, and its intrinsic expiration through ordinary replay. Implementation count is not client certification.

The held-card checkpoint reads current effective costs of the other physical hand cards. Mixed parity waits; uniform parity transforms the same physical card once. The March 26 [35.0.2 official hotfix](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/hearthstone/t/3502-hotfix-patch/158856) specifically fixes transformation after cost changes. This supports current costs rather than printed costs.

The Battlecry now leaves a replacement or already upgraded primary power unchanged. It upgrades a basic primary power, makes the new power cost one, refreshes its use and leaves a secondary power untouched. The original staged implementation incorrectly overwrote any primary replacement. A firsthand [April 2 Imbue interaction report](https://www.reddit.com/r/hearthstone/comments/1sao302/shoudnt_genn_unimbue_the_hero_power/) observes that Genn does not restore the base power after Imbue. This report supports the repair but is not developer confirmation or a frozen-patch trace. The [April 9 already-upgraded-power report](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/hearthstone/t/hero-power-upgrade/159563) is similarly limited player evidence.

Local checks cover the eleven literal class bindings and their paid effects, cost-changing parity, physical identity, repeated upgrades, custom-power preservation, primary/secondary separation, use refresh, full-board availability, Paladin partial board space, Shaman choices and Death Knight expiration. Ordinary registry and legal constructed-deck checks use no injected outcome pool or replacement rule table.

Remaining fidelity questions are explicit in `genn_starting_power_and_parity`: empty-other-hand semantics, simultaneous held transformations, copied basic powers, already-upgraded power cost interactions, persistent cost enchantments and transformation event ordering. Empty-hand transformation currently follows the uniform-parity condition; this remains a documented assumption pending an independent trace. No training or notebook cells were run.
