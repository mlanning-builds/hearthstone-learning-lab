# Temporary cards and attached Follow effects

October 1, 2026. Candidate: `staging/rebased-88`; pinned regular Standard catalog across all 11 classes. No Mercenaries or Battlegrounds. **725 / 1,185 collectible implementations written; 460 remain.**

## Delivered shared behavior

Temporary is a physical hand-card property. At the owner's end-turn cleanup, Temporary cards are discarded through the shared discard/event machinery, including cards generated during earlier end-turn effects. Temporary cards obtained during the opponent's turn survive that turn. Copies preserve the flag; the existing clean shuffle/return transitions reset granted enchantments. The owner's observation exposes the property; the opponent's private hand remains hidden.

The next-Temporary-card discount shares the normal modifier and payment paths. It persists across ordinary plays and discards, stacks with another copy, and is consumed by playing the eligible card, including a countered spell.

Follow effects are attached to physical cards, expire at turn cleanup, and resolve in queued after-play frames. They can pass through minions, spells, weapons and locations; Battlecry and spell choices finish before the attachment resolves. Every transfer checks complete current play legality, including available mana, targets, board space and play locks. Independent payloads can coexist. Summoning a card without playing it does not trigger its attachment.

Feature schema **v17** adds Temporary status and visible attached-effect metadata. Older policy checkpoints are incompatible. No training or deck search ran.

## Rule evidence and limits

The [Temporary rules](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Temporary-related) specify owner-turn discard and discard-trigger interactions. [Cursed Catacombs](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Cursed_Catacombs) and [Follow the Fuse](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Follow_the_Fuse), together with pinned card/enchantment records, supply the declared effects.

Independent client traces remain needed for cards generated by the expiry discards themselves, copy/shuffle/return retention, attachment selection weighting and ordering, countered carriers, damage attribution and spell-damage snapshot timing. These gaps are recorded in `expanded/fidelity_gaps.json`. The engine's declared behavior is tested locally; it is not independently certified client behavior.
